01 // FSE PORTAL (DEDICATED FIELD WORKSPACE) FSE >>

Field Support Engineers verify their Site ID & Tenant ID to log diesel deliveries, view animated tank & BUB/Grid status, complete the 12-item mandatory photo PPM checklist, and submit site tickets & complaints.

02 // ADMIN PORTAL (OVERVIEW & FULL CONTROL) ADMIN >>

Administrators review the live Overview (Tickets, Complaints & Fuel Issues), accept or override FSE PPM reports, manage Light & BUB controls, calibrate live diesel levels, and dispatch Finance/Team emails.

03 // EXACT DIESEL TELEMETRY & 7-DAY ALARMS FUEL >>

Continuous daily fuel depletion math synchronized with live calendar dates for 15 kVA & 20 kVA generators, with automatic 7-day depletion email alarms to Finance, FSE, and Admin.

04 // PERSONALIZED ZERO-CLASH PPM CALENDAR PPM >>

Every tower receives its own staggered, non-clashing PPM calendar date aligned daily with live dates, paired with Admin team task email assignments.
